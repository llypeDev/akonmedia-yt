  2. ffprobe reads it, ffmpeg cuts a poster and an adaptive HLS ladder (up to 1080p / 720p / 480p) plus seek previews.
  3. Audio gets a waveform cover, images a thumbnail, PDFs a first-page preview.
  4. When it is ready, the page refreshes itself and the player picks the best rendition for each viewer.
